How can we help?

Questions about CleanReport or a preview build? Contact the developer directly.

What to include

  • Your CleanReport version/build and iOS version, if available.
  • What you tried, what you expected, and the exact error message.
  • Steps that reproduce the problem. Attach a redacted screenshot only if it helps.

Please remove client names, property addresses, faces, access codes and other private details. Never send passwords or payment-card details. For diagnostics, agree on a redacted screenshot or specific non-sensitive test file first; do not send original customer photos or complete backups.

Common questions

Why is a room still incomplete?

Each applicable area needs one readable Before photo and one readable After photo. A missing, damaged or inaccessible local copy keeps that slot incomplete. Import a usable replacement, then run the readiness check again.

A room does not exist at this property.

Mark the area N/A and enter a reason. N/A keeps any existing photos for later reuse; it does not delete them. At least one area must be applicable to pass the report readiness check.

Why does a photo need an internet connection?

The app works with local photos, but a selected image that exists only in iCloud may need Apple’s system picker to download it. Choose a photo already available on the device or retry when the original can be downloaded.

Can I use the app without camera access?

You can choose an existing photo through the system picker. To take a new photo, allow camera access in iPhone Settings. CleanReport does not need microphone or location access.

Why can I not create another active job?

The Free policy allows two active jobs at a time. Archive a job to free an active slot. Archiving keeps its data; it is not deletion. Pro purchasing is not yet available in the current development build.

Is my data synchronized or recoverable by support?

There is no CleanReport account or app-managed cloud sync. The developer cannot retrieve your local jobs or photos. Device backups depend on your Apple settings. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot a storage problem; contact support first. In-app backup/restore is still in development.

Does “ready” mean a customer received a report?

No. A readiness check only checks the required documentation. It does not certify cleanliness, image authenticity or delivery. PDF export is being developed; future sharing will use the destination you choose in the system share sheet.
