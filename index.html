For iPhone · Local-first

A clear record of every clean.

CleanReport is designed for independent move-out and short-term-rental cleaners: organize before-and-after photos by room, check for missing evidence, and prepare a completion report.

Get support ↗Read the privacy policy
Download CleanReport on the App Store. You can unlock Pro with a one-time purchase in Settings or restore a previous purchase there.

01 / ORGANIZE

One room at a time.

Keep selected or captured photos with the job they belong to. Start with six standard areas and add rooms when needed.

02 / CHECK

See what is missing.

Required Before and After slots make missing photos visible. A room that does not apply needs a reason.

03 / REPORT

Prepare a useful handoff.

Create a local PDF report after the required checks pass. A completeness check documents required photos; it does not certify cleaning quality.
